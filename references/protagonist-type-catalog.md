# Protagonist Type Catalog

Use this catalog to classify Korean webnovel protagonists and design growth curves, position emphasis, reward structures, crisis intensity, repeated-reward risks, and recommended prices or reversals.

Combine one primary type with one or two modifiers when needed. Example: "regression optimizer + hidden overpowered academy student + guild disruptor."

## Quick Combination Rules

- For instant catharsis: choose a hidden overpowered or early burst type, then add public consequence within 1-3 episodes.
- For long paid serialization: pair a strong capability curve with slower status or resource growth.
- For romance fantasy: make reputation, household authority, and witness control as important as competence.
- For finance or management: make rewards material and legible through ownership, contracts, cashflow, governance, or public fear.
- For regression: invalidate future knowledge through changed loyalties, prices, injuries, timing, or institutions.
- For extra survival: convert repeated danger into permanent safety upgrades so the reader feels progress.
- For munchkin protagonists: move the main conflict from "can they win?" to "who reacts, controls, fears, frames, or depends on the win?"
