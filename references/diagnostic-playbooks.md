# Diagnostic Playbooks

Use this reference when a user asks why a protagonist growth curve feels flat, too easy, too frustrating, too slow, too scattered, or hard to serialize.

## Quick Diagnosis

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Minimal Repair | Do Not Do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 주인공이 너무 쉽게 이김 | conflict stays in raw power output | move pressure to exposure, control, law, collateral damage, or social cost | add a stronger enemy with no setup |
| 성장감이 안 보임 | rewards are numeric or abstract | make the next scene change through rank, witness, asset, relationship, or access | add another stat increase |
| 보상이 반복됨 | every win gives the same category | rotate capability, status, resource, and burden relief rewards | stack similar skills |
| 고구마가 길어짐 | protagonist has no concrete progress | add a small irreversible gain every 1-3 episodes | delay all payoff to the arc ending |
| 사이다가 약함 | antagonist loses privately or vaguely | add a public witness, material loss, rank shift, or forced apology | explain victory only in narration |
| 회귀 지식이 사기임 | future knowledge stays perfectly accurate | change prices, loyalties, injury timing, market conditions, or hidden variables | make the protagonist forget randomly |
| 빙의자가 너무 편함 | original body's debts vanish | bring back past victims, contracts, family reputation, or identity tests | excuse all past behavior |
| 먼치킨 긴장감이 죽음 | victory answers every question | make the question "who reacts to the victory?" | nerf the protagonist arbitrarily |
| 라이벌이 멍청함 | rival repeats the same failed tactic | let the rival learn one true fact and misread one key fact | make the rival omniscient |
| 중반부가 늘어짐 | next ceiling is invisible | seed forbidden zones, veteran failures, institutional ranks, prices, or rumors | introduce a new continent abruptly |

## Repair By Protagonist Type

| Type | Common Failure | Strong Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 먼치킨 | no one can threaten them | make power public enough to attract ownership, law, worship, fear, or hostages |
| 성장형 | training feels repetitive | attach training to a deadline, rival schedule, injury risk, public test, or scarce material |
| 회귀자 | knows too much | make early changes corrupt future maps, prices, allies, and enemy timing |
| 빙의자 | survival route becomes checklist | make the possessed body's history demand payment |
| 천재형 | effort and cost disappear | add expectation, exploitation, envy, burnout, or a social blind spot |
| 노력형 | early episodes lack hook | give one special constraint, rival, or visible micro-win before long training |
| 악역영애 | everyone forgives too fast | preserve victims, rumors, etiquette traps, household politics, and apology cost |
| 엑스트라 생존형 | protagonist stays passive | convert survival into permanent safety tools, rules, allies, or leverage |
| 재벌/경영형 | money solves everything | use liquidity, governance, regulation, media, voting rights, and betrayal |
| 제작/요리형 | product-of-the-week fatigue | make supply chains, copycats, patents, customers, and safety matter |

## Arc-Level Repair Patterns

| Episode Range | If It Fails | Repair Move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-5 | premise is unclear | show the signature advantage or wound in action immediately |
| 6-15 | engine feels random | repeat the core win pattern with a new cost |
| 16-25 | paid conversion lacks force | give an irreversible public shift and a larger institution's reaction |
| 26-50 | middle sags | expand board through faction, territory, company, team, guild, sect, or court |
| 51-100 | protagonist repeats old wins | make old shortcuts dangerous in the new arena |
| 100+ | scale is too large to care | anchor mythic stakes to vows, disciples, family, team, territory, or legacy |

## Reward Rotation Checklist

Before adding a new reward, check the previous three rewards:

| If Last Rewards Were | Next Reward Should Be |
| --- | --- |
| skill, skill, skill | status, public witness, or resource gate |
| money, money, money | authority, legal risk, or relationship pressure |
| praise, praise, praise | obligation, rival adaptation, or moral price |
| survival, survival, survival | permanent safety upgrade or counterattack option |
| revenge, revenge, revenge | information, restoration, ally, or self-definition |
| romance approval, romance approval, romance approval | household power, political cost, or external threat |

## Public Consequence Ladder

Use this ladder to decide how visible a reward should become:

| Visibility | Use When | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Secret | early hidden power or survival | confidence grows, danger is delayed |
| One witness | need tension without full reveal | witness can protect, blackmail, recruit, or fear |
| Small group | academy, party, household, team | ranking and social pressure begin |
| Institution | guild, sect, court, company, agency | law, contracts, office, and politics activate |
| Public myth | season-level transformation | worship, fear, copycats, enemies, and loss of privacy |
