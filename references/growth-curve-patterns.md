# Growth Curve Patterns

Use these patterns to choose how the protagonist's power, status, resources, and burden should move across a Korean serialized webnovel.

## Pattern Index

| Pattern | Best For | Core Pleasure | Main Risk | Tension Tool |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early Burst | hunter, tower, revenge, possession | immediate proof of superiority | ceiling collapses | public consequence and new stage |
| Hidden Overpowered | academy, murim, extra survival | underestimated catharsis | reveal delay fatigue | forced partial reveals |
| Staircase Growth | training, sports, academy, murim | fair effort and milestone satisfaction | slow early hook | frequent micro-wins |
| Compound Accumulation | finance, management, crafting, cooking | small choices becoming huge | abstract rewards | visible assets and enemies |
| Delayed Reward | revenge, villainess, survival | patience and payoff | frustration | small proof and future promise |
| Costly Exchange | system, constellation, dark fantasy | power with dread | over-punishment | reader-visible bargain logic |
| Regression Optimization | regression, second chance, finance | future knowledge exploitation | omniscience flattening | butterfly effects and unknowns |
| Reputation Reversal | villainess, disgraced genius, exile | public misunderstanding corrected | repetitive humiliation | escalating witnesses |
| Authority Expansion | chaebol, guild, kingdom, sect | command range widens | bureaucracy slows pace | rival institutions |
| Team Amplifier | idol, sports, management, party raid | protagonist raises others | protagonist fades | signature decision moments |
| Survival Climb | extra, apocalypse, palace, prison | living one more day | misery fatigue | concrete safety upgrades |
| Mythic Ascension | epic fantasy, murim, constellation | identity becomes legend | distance from human stakes | personal vows and public worship cost |

## Episode Rhythm

| Range | Primary Job | Reward | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-3 | Prove the fantasy and wound | signature edge, first reversal, first survival | suspicion, debt, enemy attention |
| 4-10 | Establish repeatable engine | skill use pattern, ally, small asset, rank hint | limitation, rival data, social trap |
| 11-25 | Deliver first arc transformation | public win, title, artifact, contract, revenge step | institution reacts, new ceiling appears |
| 26-50 | Expand board | faction access, territory, team, capital, secret truth | obligations, media, family, guild, sect, law |
| 51-100 | Reprice old power | stronger enemies, moral choices, larger operations | past shortcuts become liabilities |
| 100+ | Transform identity | myth, office, ownership, throne, master rank | world-level enemies and legacy stakes |

## Unlock Timing

Use this timing unless the genre promise requires a faster reveal:

- Episode 1: show the signature advantage or its absence as a wound.
- Episodes 2-3: give the first use case and first limitation.
- Episodes 5-8: add a second application, not a second unrelated power.
- Episodes 10-15: make the advantage public to at least one dangerous observer.
- Episodes 20-30: upgrade the advantage by paying a cost or changing position.
- Episodes 40-60: introduce a counter that cannot be solved by raw output.
- Episodes 80-120: force the protagonist to delegate, govern, teach, invest, or sacrifice.

## Crisis Intensity Dial

| If Protagonist Feels | Add | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Too weak | small reliable competence, one visible win, a controllable tool | random rescue |
| Too strong | public cost, legal limit, hostage, market scarcity, rival adaptation | bigger enemy with no setup |
| Too lucky | prerequisite, prior clue, partial failure | reward with no price |
| Too isolated | ally with need and leverage | passive follower collection |
| Too rich | liquidity, governance, regulation, betrayal, public optics | infinite spending |
| Too famous | scrutiny, stalkers, sponsors, anti-fans, political capture | fame only as applause |
| Too hidden | forced witness, contract, rank test, rumor | endless secret convenience |
| Too miserable | concrete safe zone, ally warmth, small luxury | nonstop deprivation |

## Reward Structure By Curve

| Curve | Reward Examples | Price Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capability | skill, technique, recipe, strategy, body upgrade, performance ability | cooldown, injury, taboo, compatibility, exposure |
| Status | rank, title, debut, noble recognition, guild post, sect authority | public scrutiny, political enemy, expectation |
| Resources | money, shares, artifact, team, facility, territory, IP | debt, tax, theft, governance, maintenance |
| Burden | curse reduced, debt delayed, enemy removed, secret protected | new promise, moral stain, stronger watcher |

## Repair Moves

- If rewards repeat, switch the next reward category: power to status, status to resource, resource to burden relief.
- If the ceiling appears suddenly, seed it through rumor, failed veteran, forbidden zone, market price, exam record, or old scar.
- If training drags, attach public stakes, a rival schedule, bodily risk, or a scarce resource.
- If future knowledge solves everything, make changed events invalidate maps, prices, loyalties, and enemy timing.
- If the protagonist is a munchkin, make the question "who controls or fears this power?" instead of "can they win?"
- If the protagonist is a survival extra, make the question "what safety can be made permanent?" instead of "will they die today?"
