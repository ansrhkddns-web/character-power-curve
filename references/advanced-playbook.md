# Advanced Playbook

Use this reference when tension drops, an arc needs a stronger reward calendar, or the protagonist has become too powerful for the current conflict arena.

## Four-Curve Balance

| Capability | Status | Resources | Burden | Tension State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Up | Up | Up | Flat | Wish fulfillment is high, danger may flatten |
| Up | Down | Mixed | Up | Strong hidden-power tension |
| Up | Up | Down | Up | Public rise with scarcity |
| Flat | Up | Up | Up | Political/social pressure |
| Up | Flat | Up | Up | Good for finance, management, crafting, or production arcs |
| Flat | Down | Up | Up | Survival, exile, villainess, or extra tension |
| Up | Down | Down | Up | Strong revenge or fugitive engine |

At least one curve should resist each major win.

## Reward Calendar

Use this cadence for serial satisfaction:

- Every episode: micro shift or concrete question.
- Every 2-3 episodes: visible reward, public reaction, or meaningful setback.
- Every 5-8 episodes: new tool, new rival response, new ceiling.
- Every 10-15 episodes: arc reward with lasting burden.
- Every 25 episodes: identity/status transformation or world-board expansion.

## Counterplay Bank

Counter a strong protagonist with:

- Information asymmetry.
- Public law, noble protocol, guild rules, sect law, idol contracts, league rules, or market regulation.
- Hostages, contracts, debts, witnesses, family obligations, sponsorship terms, or fandom optics.
- Terrain, compatibility, cooldowns, resource scarcity, injury, taboo, or time windows.
- Rival team composition.
- Reputation traps.
- Moral constraints.
- Future knowledge decay.
- Market liquidity, governance, taxes, media leaks, or political capture.

## Progression Failure Modes

- Reward duplication: new skill solves the same problem as an old skill.
- Invisible growth: numbers rise but scenes do not change.
- Ceiling whiplash: stronger enemy appears without prior signal.
- Cost amnesia: injury, debt, cooldown, contract, scandal, or taboo vanishes.
- Rival stupidity: enemy repeats a failed tactic.
- Burden collapse: every gain removes problems instead of changing them.
- Position stagnation: the protagonist wins but their social/world position stays identical.
- Genre drift: reward type no longer matches the promised fantasy.

## Repair Recipes

| Problem | Minimal Repair |
| --- | --- |
| Munchkin tension collapse | Move conflict from output to control, legitimacy, secrecy, law, or collateral damage |
| Weak payoff | Give a visible public witness, status marker, or material asset |
| Repeated humiliation | Convert the next win into irreversible leverage |
| Overloaded power list | Merge powers into one signature advantage with broader applications |
| Passive allies | Give allies needs, leverage, and a reason to disagree |
| Flat rival | Let the rival learn one true thing and misread one important thing |
| Abstract finance reward | Show ownership, cashflow, legal control, or who becomes afraid |
| Slow training | Attach deadline, rival schedule, injury risk, or public test |
