## Genre Starter Shapes

### Hunter/Gate

- Opening proof: protagonist survives or clears a situation their measured rank says they should not.
- Position emphasis: hidden variable becomes a recruitment or containment target.
- Reward order: survival proof -> rare drop -> party/guild attention -> public rank contradiction.
- First price: measurement anomaly, guild contract pressure, or a rescued witness who spreads rumor.
- Next ceiling: disaster gate, raid commander politics, or monster ecology that counters raw output.

### Tower Climb

- Opening proof: protagonist sees the floor rule behind the obvious trial.
- Position emphasis: rule predator or administrator anomaly.
- Reward order: hidden condition -> floor privilege -> unusual title -> rival team data.
- First price: patched rules, admin attention, or a floor where the exploit hurts an ally.
- Next ceiling: social floors, contract floors, or floors with incomplete information.

### Academy

- Opening proof: low-ranking student wins a test, duel, project, or social exchange through a signature edge.
- Position emphasis: underestimated student, defective genius, or transfer variable.
- Reward order: private proof -> teacher notice -> peer witness -> rank movement.
- First price: sabotage, rumor, special exam, or forced team assignment.
- Next ceiling: institution faction, family pressure, scholarship condition, or public tournament.

### Murim

- Opening proof: body, meridian, footwork, or technique principle changes the expected duel result.
- Position emphasis: sect exile, hidden lineage, forbidden disciple, or broken body with new path.
- Reward order: restored body clue -> first technique -> public duel -> manual/elder attention.
- First price: qi backlash, sect law, inherited enemy, or taboo manual compatibility.
- Next ceiling: orthodox/demonic politics, clan hostage rule, or master-level witness.

### Romance Fantasy/Villainess

- Opening proof: protagonist avoids one death flag without making everyone like her.
- Position emphasis: hated name with strategic competence.
- Reward order: survival -> witness -> household leverage -> public reputation crack.
- First price: past victim, etiquette trap, contract clause, or family debt.
- Next ceiling: court faction, marriage politics, saintess trial, or inheritance law.

### Chaebol/Finance

- Opening proof: protagonist converts future knowledge or operator skill into a legally visible asset.
- Position emphasis: suspicious heir, market disruptor, or invisible operator.
- Reward order: small asset -> voting/contract leverage -> board attention -> media signal.
- First price: audit trail, liquidity issue, family suspicion, or regulator interest.
- Next ceiling: governance, hostile M&A, political tie, or public scandal.

### Idol/Actor

- Opening proof: one stage, audition, take, or production decision creates public evidence.
- Position emphasis: comeback candidate, hidden genius, or edited public sinner.
- Reward order: clip reaction -> staff notice -> fan seed -> contract/role leverage.
- First price: body limit, malicious edit, agency control, or fandom split.
- Next ceiling: live broadcast, scandal resurfacing, rival team, or role that bleeds into identity.

### Apocalypse/Survival

- Opening proof: protagonist turns knowledge or caution into a concrete safe tool.
- Position emphasis: weak survivor becoming safety architect.
- Reward order: safe room -> tool -> ally -> repeatable rule.
- First price: resource scarcity, group suspicion, or saved person who brings danger.
- Next ceiling: governance, refugees, winter/famine, or enemy group with better legitimacy.

## Default First-Arc Recipe

Use this if the user gives almost no detail:

| Episode Range | Function | Concrete Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-2 | prove wound and edge | show why the protagonist should lose, then show the signature exception |
| 3-5 | define limitation | win again, but leave a witness, cost, cooldown, debt, or rumor |
| 6-10 | create repeatable engine | let the protagonist solve a second problem through the same core advantage |
| 11-15 | force public consequence | rank, contract, duel, article, audit, invitation, or accusation |
| 16-25 | paid-conversion turn | irreversible status shift plus larger institution reaction |

## Preset Selection Rule

When multiple presets fit:

1. Choose the genre with the clearest reader reward in episode 1.
2. Use the protagonist's origin to choose the second preset.
3. Use the main arena of conflict to choose the crisis preset.
4. If the request includes romance, family, sect, guild, agency, or company, add the corresponding institutional pressure even if the core genre is combat.
