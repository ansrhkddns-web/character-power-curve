# Archetype Combination Matrix

Use this reference when one catalog type is not enough. Build a Korean webnovel protagonist from four layers:

1. Origin: why the protagonist starts outside the normal order.
2. Advantage: what lets the protagonist win differently.
3. Position: how the world sees or misreads the protagonist.
4. Deficiency: what prevents the fantasy from becoming flat.

## Layer 1: Origin

| Origin | Best Genres | Built-In Promise | Built-In Problem |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 회귀자 | hunter, finance, revenge, sports, idol | knows the board early | future knowledge decays |
| 빙의자 | villainess, extra, academy, chaebol | knows role and death flags | identity and past-owner debt |
| 환생자 | fantasy, murim, academy | maturity and second life | legitimacy and emotional distance |
| 추방자 | murim, fantasy, guild, idol | revenge and proof | no protection or resources |
| 몰락 귀족/재벌 | rofan, chaebol, kingdom | restoration fantasy | inherited enemies and debt |
| 엑스트라 | academy, apocalypse, novel possession | survival through meta-knowledge | low authority and plot proximity |
| 천재 | academy, sports, magic, idol | quick proof of superiority | envy, exploitation, isolation |
| 노력형 | sports, murim, academy | fair growth satisfaction | early hook must be supported |
| 저주/결함 보유자 | fantasy, murim, villainess | defect becomes weapon | cost must remain visible |
| 관리자/후방직 | management, idol, raid, territory | makes others stronger | protagonist must still decide key turns |

## Layer 4: Deficiency

| Deficiency | Use When | Good Price | Bad Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 시간 제한 | plot needs urgency | deadline, cooldown, scheduled death | fake countdown with no consequence |
| 신체 손상 | power needs weight | injury management, relapse | miracle cure too early |
| 사회적 불신 | status arc needed | witnesses, contracts, apology | repetitive public humiliation |
| 법/계약 제한 | strong protagonist needs brakes | clauses, trial, audit, guild law | boring paperwork only |
| 자원 병목 | crafting/finance needs tension | material scarcity, cashflow, logistics | arbitrary shortage |
| 관계 약점 | emotional stakes needed | hostage, disappointment, trust cost | melodrama unrelated to plot |
| 정보 오염 | future knowledge too strong | changed timeline, fake data | random twist |
| 도덕 비용 | revenge/taboo power needs depth | collateral, apology, sacrifice | sermon without choice |
| 공개 위험 | hidden power too easy | rumor, witness, test, leak | secret stays convenient forever |
| 통치 책임 | authority arc needed | casualties, taxation, delegation | bureaucracy with no payoff |

## Paid-Serialization Reward Presets

| Episode Range | Reader Needs | Good Reward | Necessary Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1-5 | immediate promise | first reversal, signature edge, survival proof | suspicion or limitation |
| 6-15 | repeatable engine | first ally, first asset, first public witness | rival learns something |
| 16-25 | paid-conversion pressure | irreversible status shift or enemy humiliation | larger institution reacts |
| 26-50 | expanded board | faction, company, team, territory, tournament entry | obligations and resource pressure |
| 51-100 | anti-fatigue | new arena, changed rules, upgraded rival | old shortcut becomes risky |
| 100+ | myth and governance | office, title, legend, ownership, disciple/team | legacy, succession, world-level price |

## Candidate Selection Rule

When choosing between possible types:

1. Pick the type that makes the first episode strongest.
2. Reject any type whose rewards all fall into one curve.
3. Prefer the type with the clearest public consequence.
4. Prefer a deficiency that appears naturally from the genre, not an unrelated handicap.
5. Make the first major reward solve one immediate problem and create one larger problem.
